# EMAH — Physical / Financial Invariants

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-12 / ACCEPTED**

### EMAH-INV-FIN-001

**Physical Effect != Economic Effect**

Physical-energy effect SHALL remain distinct from economic effect.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-002

**Physical Delivery != Financial Settlement**

Physical delivery SHALL not be inferred from settlement.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-003

**Payment != Energy Delivery**

Payment SHALL NOT establish physical delivery.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-004

**Physical Partial Effect != Financial Partial Effect**

Physical partial effect SHALL remain distinct from financial partial effect.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-005

**EnergyEffectResidual != Financial Residual**

Physical-effect residual SHALL remain distinct from financial residual.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-006

**Settlement != Authority**

Settlement SHALL NOT create execution authority.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-007

**Physical Effect Equality != Settlement Finality**

Observed physical equality between intended and observed effect SHALL NOT
establish financial finality.

### EMAH-INV-FIN-008

**ReserveDesignation != Execution Authority**

Reserve designation SHALL NOT create execution authority.
