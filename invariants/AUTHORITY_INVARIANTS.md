# EMAH — Authority / Admissibility Invariants

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-12 / ACCEPTED**

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-001

**Capability != Authority**

Technical ability to act SHALL NOT independently establish execution authority.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-002

**Authority != Admissibility**

Valid authority SHALL NOT independently establish current admissibility.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-003

**Prior Authority != Current Execution Authority**

Historical authority SHALL NOT silently become current authority.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-004

**Prior Authorization != Present Execution Eligibility**

Previously authorized state SHALL NOT independently establish present execution
eligibility.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-005

**Credential Possession != Authority**

Possession of a credential, key, token, or control access SHALL NOT
independently establish authority.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-006

**Physical Availability != Authority**

Available energy or capacity SHALL NOT independently establish authority.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-007

**Evidence != Authority**

No evidence state SHALL independently create execution authority.

### EMAH-INV-AUTH-008

**Obligation != Authority**

Existence of an obligation SHALL NOT create authority to satisfy it.
