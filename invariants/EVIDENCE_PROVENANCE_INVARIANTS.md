# EMAH — Evidence / Provenance Invariants

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-12-P01 / CANDIDATE**

### EMAH-INV-EVD-001

**Evidence != Authority**

Evidence SHALL NOT create authority.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-002

**Evidence Present != Evidence Admissible**

Presence SHALL NOT automatically establish evidentiary admissibility.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-003

**Evidence Admissible != Evidence Sufficient**

Admissibility SHALL NOT automatically establish sufficiency.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-004

**Provenance != Truth**

Complete provenance SHALL NOT independently establish correctness.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-005

**Derived Evidence != Independent Observation**

Derived evidence SHALL preserve its derivation status.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-006

**Reconstructed Provenance != Preserved Provenance**

Reconstructed provenance SHALL remain distinguishable from continuously
preserved provenance.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-007

**Reconstruction != Preservation**

Reconstruction SHALL NOT be represented as continuous preservation.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-008

**Reconstructed Record != Original Observation**

A reconstructed record SHALL remain distinguishable from its original
observation source.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-009

**Complete Reconstruction Within Scope != Global Complete Reconstruction**

Scoped reconstruction completeness SHALL NOT be generalized globally.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-010

**Execution Block != Proof of Block**

Execution prevention SHALL remain distinct from governed refusal evidence.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-011

**Proof of Block != Execution Authority**

Proof of Block SHALL NOT create execution authority.

### EMAH-INV-EVD-012

**Certificate != Physical Energy Identity**

Certificate evidence SHALL NOT be treated as persistent physical-energy
identity.
