# EMAH — Recovery / Continuity Invariants

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-12 / ACCEPTED**

### EMAH-INV-REC-001

**Residual != Recoverable**

Residual existence SHALL NOT independently establish recoverability.

### EMAH-INV-REC-002

**Recoverable != Recovered**

Recoverability SHALL NOT be treated as completed recovery.

### EMAH-INV-REC-003

**Recovered != Requalified**

Recovery SHALL NOT independently establish requalification.

### EMAH-INV-REC-004

**Requalified != Reintegrated**

Requalification SHALL NOT independently establish reintegration.

### EMAH-INV-REC-005

**Reintegrated != Authorized for renewed consequential use**

Reintegration SHALL NOT independently create renewed execution authority.

### EMAH-INV-REC-006

**Recovery != Restoration**

Recovery SHALL NOT imply restoration of the prior historical condition.

### EMAH-INV-REC-007

**Recovery != Historical Erasure**

Recovery SHALL preserve originating historical lineage.

### EMAH-INV-REC-008

**Reintegration != Historical Reset**

Reintegration SHALL NOT erase prior residual, execution, or recovery history.

### EMAH-INV-REC-009

**EnergyEffectResidual != EnergyResidual**

Physical-effect mismatch residual SHALL remain distinct from AHME
recovery-domain residual.

### EMAH-INV-REC-010

**Remaining Capacity != Recoverability**

Residual capacity SHALL NOT independently establish recoverability.

### EMAH-INV-REC-011

**Energy Continuity != Rule Identity Continuity**

Physical-energy continuity SHALL remain distinct from EIP execution-rule
continuity.

### EMAH-INV-REC-012

**Identity-Relevant Preservation != Identity Continuity**

Preservation relevant to identity SHALL NOT independently establish identity
continuity.
