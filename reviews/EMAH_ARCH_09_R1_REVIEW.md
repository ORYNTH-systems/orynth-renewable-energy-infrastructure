# EMAH-ARCH-09-R1 — Evidence + Provenance Acceptance Review

## Disposition

**ACCEPTED**

## Reviewed Candidate

`EMAH-ARCH-09-P01`

## Findings

### R01 — Submission State

PASS.

ARCH-09-P01 was submitted with ARCH-10 closed.

### R02 — Evidence Class Registry

PASS.

Ten distinct EMAH evidence classes are admitted.

### R03 — Evidence / Authority Separation

PASS.

The architecture preserves:

**Evidence != Authority**

Evidence existence, admissibility, or sufficiency does not independently
create execution authority.

### R04 — Evidence-Class Separation

PASS.

Authority, admissibility, execution, physical-effect, recovery,
requalification, reintegration, attribute, continuity, and provenance
evidence remain distinct evidentiary classes.

### R05 — Provenance Boundary

PASS.

Provenance establishes source and derivation structure.

It does not independently establish:

- authority;
- truth;
- admissibility.

### R06 — Observation Boundary

PASS.

Observation remains distinct from state determination.

Absence of observation remains distinct from observation of zero.

Conflicting observations remain visible rather than being silently erased.

### R07 — Lineage Preservation

PASS.

The architecture preserves lineage across:

- object;
- transition;
- authority/admissibility references;
- command;
- execution;
- physical effect;
- effect residual;
- AHME residual;
- recoverability;
- recovery;
- requalification;
- reintegration;
- regenerative return.

### R08 — Branching Lineage

PASS.

Lineage is not restricted to one linear chain.

Multiple observations, recovery attempts, and downstream records may retain
their own branches.

### R09 — Reconstruction / Preservation Separation

PASS.

The architecture preserves:

**Reconstruction != Preservation**

**Reconstructed Provenance != Preserved Provenance**

A complete reconstruction within scope does not establish global completeness.

### R10 — Proof of Block Boundary

PASS.

Execution blocking remains distinct from Proof of Block.

Proof of Block retains governed refusal-evidence ownership and does not
become execution authority.

### R11 — Certificate Boundary

PASS.

Certificate or attribute provenance does not establish persistent physical
energy identity.

### R12 — Financial Evidence Boundary

PASS.

Settlement evidence remains distinct from physical-delivery evidence.

### R13 — Unknown / Conflict Preservation

PASS.

Missing, conflicting, and uncertain evidence states remain explicit.

### R14 — Historical Non-Erasure

PASS.

Evidence and reconstruction architecture does not erase prior execution,
effect, residual, or recovery history.

### R15 — Forward Boundary

PASS.

ARCH-09 does not resolve generic portfolio-wide obligation ownership.

EMAH-ARCH-10 is authorized to define the bounded energy-domain obligation
architecture while preserving that unresolved generic ownership boundary.

## Forward Constraint

EMAH-ARCH-10 may define energy-domain obligations attached to EMAH objects,
transitions, residuals, recovery, reserve designations, and consequential
effects.

It may not:

- claim portfolio-wide ownership of a generic Obligation primitive;
- infer obligation from evidence alone;
- infer authority from obligation;
- infer admissibility from obligation;
- allow compensation to self-authorize;
- allow recovery to extinguish obligations merely because physical state
  improved;
- erase obligations through reconstruction;
- collapse physical obligation into financial settlement obligation;
- preempt cross-domain interface work reserved for ARCH-11.
