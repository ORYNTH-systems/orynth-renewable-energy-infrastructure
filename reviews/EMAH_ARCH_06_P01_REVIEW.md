## Required Review Questions

### R01 — UAA Ownership

Does EMAH bind to authority without redefining generic UAA authority?

### R02 — Capability / Authority Separation

Does technical, physical, credential, or control capability remain
insufficient to establish authority?

### R03 — Transition-Specific Binding

Is authority bounded to the relevant subject, resource, action,
scope, and time?

### R04 — AOMS Ownership

Does present admissibility use current-state reconciliation without
redefining AOMS?

### R05 — Historical / Current Separation

Are prior authorization and historical admissibility kept separate from
present execution eligibility?

### R06 — Observed Event Separation

Does the architecture avoid demanding execution authority for every
naturally or externally occurring physical state change?

### R07 — Unresolved Ownership Preservation

Are generic Principal, Jurisdiction, and AutonomousAgent objects still
excluded?

### R08 — Cross-Dimensional Firewall

Can physical, economic, evidence, environmental, or recovery state
create authority or admissibility by inference?

### R09 — Fail-Closed Unknowns

Do unresolved material conditions remain unresolved rather than becoming
admissible?

### R10 — Execution Firewall

Does ARCH-06 stop before command, execution, block, effect, and
partial-effect semantics?
