## Foundational Boundary

EMAH begins from the separation:

**Capability != Authority != Admissibility != Execution != Effect**

Physical, authority, economic, evidentiary, environmental-attribute, and continuity states are not presumed interchangeable.

A command, record, payment, certificate, credential, optimization result, or market instruction does not by itself establish legitimate consequential energy execution.

## EMAH / AHME Relationship

**EMAH** is the primary renewable-energy architecture.

**AHME** is the regenerative-return architecture governing residual state, recovery, requalification, reintegration, and renewed admissibility.

The acronym expansions remain intentionally OPEN until the ontology is sufficiently stable to justify canonical expansion.

## Development Rule

Architecture precedes implementation.

Explicit admission is required.
