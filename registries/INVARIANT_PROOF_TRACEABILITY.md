## Traceability Rule

Every accepted ARCH-12 invariant must map to at least one ARCH-13 proof
obligation.

P01 establishes exactly one PRIMARY obligation per accepted invariant.

Future proof decomposition may add subordinate obligations without deleting
the primary mapping.
