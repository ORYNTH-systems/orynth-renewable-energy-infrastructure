# EMAH — Obligation Satisfaction Model

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-10 / ACCEPTED**

---

# 1. Satisfaction Basis

Obligation satisfaction must be evaluated against the obligation's declared
condition and satisfaction basis.

Evidence of activity is not enough.

Therefore:

**Action Evidence != Obligation Satisfaction**

---

# 2. Full Satisfaction

An obligation is fully satisfied only when the declared satisfaction
condition is met within the applicable boundary.

---

# 3. Partial Satisfaction

Let:

`Q_req`

represent required bounded physical or procedural satisfaction.

Let:

`Q_sat`

represent observed satisfied portion where scalar comparison is valid.

For:

`0 < Q_sat < Q_req`

the obligation is partially satisfied.

Define:

`rho_obl = Q_req - Q_sat`

where scalar semantics are valid.

This residual is an obligation remainder.

It is not automatically:

- an EnergyResidual;
- an EnergyEffectResidual;
- a financial balance;
- a compensation amount.

Therefore:

**Obligation Residual != EnergyEffectResidual**

**Obligation Residual != EnergyResidual**

**Obligation Residual != Financial Residual**

---

# 4. Overperformance

Performance beyond a required physical quantity does not automatically satisfy
other independent dimensions of an obligation.

Therefore:

**Overperformance in Quantity != Universal Satisfaction**

---

# 5. Evidence Requirement

Satisfaction requires evidence appropriate to the declared obligation.

But:

**Evidence Present != Obligation Satisfied**

---

# 6. Settlement Boundary

Financial settlement does not independently satisfy a physical obligation.

Therefore:

**ECO_SETTLED != Physical Obligation Satisfied**

---

# 7. Authority Boundary

An obligation may require action.

It does not create authority to take that action.

Therefore:

**Obligation Requirement != Execution Authority**
