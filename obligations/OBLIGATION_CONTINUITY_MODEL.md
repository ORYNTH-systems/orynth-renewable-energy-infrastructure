# EMAH — Obligation Continuity Model

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-10-P01 / CANDIDATE**

Obligation continuity concerns preservation of the obligation record and
unresolved duty across state change.

---

# 1. State Change

A physical state change does not silently extinguish an obligation.

Therefore:

**Physical State Change != Obligation Extinguishment**

---

# 2. Recovery

Recovery does not automatically extinguish the obligation that arose from the
originating condition.

Therefore:

**Recovery != Obligation Extinguishment**

Any closure must be independently determined.

---

# 3. Reconstruction

A reconstructed obligation record must remain distinguishable from a
continuously preserved record.

Therefore:

**Reconstructed Obligation Record != Preserved Obligation Continuity**

---

# 4. Transfer

Transfer of an energy object does not by itself establish transfer of
obligation responsibility.

Therefore:

**Object Transfer != Obligation Transfer**

Responsibility transfer requires an explicit basis.

---

# 5. Continuity / Identity

Obligation-record continuity does not establish persistent physical energy
identity.

Therefore:

**Obligation Continuity != Physical Identity**

---

# 6. Historical Non-Erasure

Satisfied, superseded, expired, disputed, or closed obligations remain part
of historical lineage.

Therefore:

**Obligation Closure != Historical Erasure**
