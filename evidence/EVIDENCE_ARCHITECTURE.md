# EMAH — Evidence Architecture

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-09-P01 / CANDIDATE**

Evidence supports reconstruction, inspection, and defensible determination.

Evidence does not create authority.

---

# 1. Governing Rule

**Evidence != Authority**

**Evidence Present != Evidence Admissible**

**Evidence Admissible != Evidence Sufficient**

**Evidence Sufficient != Execution Authorized**

---

# 2. Evidence Classes

EMAH distinguishes:

- Authority Evidence
- Admissibility Evidence
- Execution Evidence
- Physical Effect Evidence
- Recovery Evidence
- Requalification Evidence
- Reintegration Evidence
- Attribute Evidence
- Continuity Evidence
- Provenance Evidence

These classes may reference one another.

They are not interchangeable.

---

# 3. Authority Evidence

Authority Evidence may demonstrate the existence, identity, scope,
continuity, or status of an upstream authority reference.

It does not create that authority.

Therefore:

**Authority Evidence != Authority**

---

# 4. Execution Evidence

Execution Evidence may establish:

- command presentation;
- gate evaluation;
- execution attempt;
- block;
- abort;
- failure;
- completion.

It does not independently establish physical effect.

Therefore:

**Execution Evidence != Physical Effect Evidence**

---

# 5. Physical Effect Evidence

Physical Effect Evidence concerns observed consequential physical effect.

It may include:

- measurement result;
- observation window;
- physical interface;
- quantity;
- units;
- uncertainty;
- source measurement reference.

It does not independently prove causal attribution.

Therefore:

**Effect Evidence != Proven Causation**

---

# 6. Recovery Evidence

Recovery Evidence concerns:

- recoverability evaluation;
- recovery initiation;
- recovery process;
- partial recovery;
- recovered state;
- residual remainder;
- requalification;
- reintegration.

Recovery evidence does not erase originating history.

---

# 7. Attribute Evidence

Environmental or renewable attribute evidence concerns claims,
qualification, support, provenance, and validity.

It does not establish persistent physical identity.

Therefore:

**Certificate != Physical Energy Identity**

---

# 8. Financial Boundary

Economic records may be referenced where relevant.

They remain separate from physical-energy evidence.

Therefore:

**Settlement Evidence != Physical Delivery Evidence**

---

# 9. Evidence Status

Evidence condition may include:

- present;
- absent;
- incomplete;
- conflicting;
- provenance-impaired;
- integrity-impaired;
- reconstructable;
- nonreconstructable;
- review-required.

These conditions align with accepted ARCH-04 evidentiary state semantics.

---

# 10. Forward Boundary

ARCH-09 defines evidence and provenance architecture.

It does not yet define generic obligation ownership.

That remains reserved for ARCH-10.
