# EMAH — Reconstruction Model

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-09-P01 / CANDIDATE**

Reconstruction determines what can be defensibly re-established from
retained evidence.

---

# 1. Governing Rule

**Reconstruction != Preservation**

**Reconstructed Record != Original Observation**

**Reconstruction != Historical Erasure**

---

# 2. Candidate Reconstruction Classes

- `RECON_COMPLETE_WITHIN_SCOPE`
- `RECON_PARTIAL`
- `RECON_CONFLICTING`
- `RECON_PROVENANCE_GAP`
- `RECON_INDETERMINATE`
- `RECON_IMPOSSIBLE`

---

# 3. Scope Boundary

A complete reconstruction within a declared scope does not establish
global completeness.

Therefore:

**Complete Reconstruction Within Scope != Global Complete Reconstruction**

---

# 4. Unknown Preservation

Missing evidence must remain missing.

Conflicting evidence must remain conflicting.

Uncertainty must remain uncertainty.

---

# 5. Proof of Block Boundary

Proof of Block may provide governed refusal evidence.

ARCH-09 does not redefine Proof of Block ownership or blocking semantics.

Therefore:

**Execution Block != Proof of Block**

and:

**Proof of Block != Execution Authority**

---

# 6. Reconstructed Provenance

Where provenance is reconstructed rather than continuously preserved,
that distinction must remain explicit.

Therefore:

**Reconstructed Continuity != Preserved Continuity**
