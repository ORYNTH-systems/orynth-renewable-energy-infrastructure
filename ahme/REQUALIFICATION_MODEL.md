# AHME — Requalification Model

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-08 / ACCEPTED**

Requalification determines whether recovered energy or capacity satisfies the
requirements relevant to renewed participation.

---

# 1. Governing Chain

**Recovered != Requalified**

---

# 2. Candidate States

ARCH-04 defines:

- `REC_REQUALIFICATION_REQUIRED`
- `REC_REQUALIFIED`
- `REC_REQUALIFICATION_FAILED`

---

# 3. Requalification Basis

Candidate requalification may evaluate:

- physical condition;
- carrier;
- quantity;
- quality;
- measurement boundary;
- environmental attribute validity where relevant;
- continuity condition;
- storage / transfer compatibility;
- other domain-specific qualification requirements.

---

# 4. Qualification / Authority Separation

Requalification does not create execution authority.

Therefore:

**REC_REQUALIFIED != AUTH_VALID**

and:

**REC_REQUALIFIED != Present Execution Eligibility**

---

# 5. Attribute Boundary

Requalification may depend upon renewable or environmental qualification.

Such qualification does not create persistent physical energy identity.

Therefore:

**Requalification != Persistent Physical Identity**
