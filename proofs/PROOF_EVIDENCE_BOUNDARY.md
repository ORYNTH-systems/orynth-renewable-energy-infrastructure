# EMAH — Proof / Evidence Boundary

## Status

**EMAH-ARCH-13 / ACCEPTED**

Evidence is necessary for many proof obligations.

Evidence is not interchangeable with proof.

---

# 1. Governing Distinctions

**Evidence != Proof**

**Evidence Integrity != Proposition Truth**

**Auditability != Proof**

**Reconstruction != Proof**

**Proof of Block != Proof of Global Correctness**

---

# 2. Evidence Sufficiency

Evidence must be evaluated relative to the proposition being tested.

Therefore:

**Evidence Sufficient for Proposition A != Evidence Sufficient for Proposition B**

---

# 3. Provenance

Strong provenance increases inspectability.

It does not independently prove the proposition.

Therefore:

**Provenance != Proof**

---

# 4. Hash / Integrity Boundary

Hash equality or tamper evidence may establish byte identity or integrity
within scope.

It does not establish semantic correctness.

Therefore:

**Hash Match != Semantic Correctness**

---

# 5. Execution Evidence

Execution evidence may establish what execution occurred.

It does not independently prove that execution was authorized, admissible, or
correct.

Therefore:

**Execution Evidence != Legitimate Execution Proof**

---

# 6. Recovery Evidence

Recovery evidence may establish recovery events or states.

It does not independently prove that recovery preserved every required
continuity or obligation invariant.
